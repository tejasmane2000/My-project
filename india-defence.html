01 / The services

Three services. One posture.

Each service brings a distinct role to national defence, supported by specialised commands and an expanding emphasis on coordination.

01

Indian Army

Responsible for land-based operations and the defence of India's extensive borders, including demanding high-altitude terrain.

02

Indian Navy

Safeguards maritime interests, supports presence across the Indian Ocean, and helps keep critical sea routes secure.

03

Indian Air Force

Provides air defence and air power, with a central role in surveillance, mobility, and support across theatres.
